HCOS™ Foundation Paper 1
Love Is the Beginning
Executive Summary
Every healthy human system begins with love.
This paper introduces the foundational principle of Human-Centered Operating Systems™ (HCOS™): that love is the starting point for designing healthy relationships, organizations, communities, technologies, and societies.
Within HCOS™, love is understood as an intentional commitment to recognize the inherent dignity of every person while pursuing truth, reducing unnecessary suffering, and creating the conditions in which people can flourish.
This foundation establishes the philosophical basis upon which the remaining HCOS™ Foundations, Standards, Operating Model, and implementation tools are built.
